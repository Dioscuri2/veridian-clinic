# T4: Discovery Call Script (30 Minutes)

**Purpose:** Consultative GP call to qualify fit, build trust, and convert to Metabolic Turnaround booking  
**Tone:** Clinical and warm. GP-to-patient conversation, not sales call.  
**Key principle:** Lead with curiosity. Listen first. The programme sells itself when the right person understands it.  
**Booking tool:** ThanksDoc

---

## BEFORE THE CALL (2 min prep)

Review:
- How they came to us (quiz / referral / LinkedIn / organic search)
- Quiz result if available (mAge, band, weakest area)
- Any pre-call form notes if collected

Open their name in ThanksDoc so you can add consultation notes immediately.

## CALL STRUCTURE

### 0:00-3:00 — OPENING (3 minutes)

"Hi [name], this is Dr Tosin calling from Veridian Clinic. Thanks for taking the time to speak today.

I want to be clear from the start: this is not a sales call. It is a conversation for me to understand what is going on for you, and for you to decide whether what we do is actually the right fit. If it is not, I will tell you that.

Can I start by asking what brought you to us? What is the main thing you are trying to understand or change?"

[LISTEN. Do not interrupt. Let them describe the problem fully.]

---

### 3:00-15:00 — CLINICAL HISTORY (12 minutes)

Work through these questions naturally, not as a checklist:

**Energy and symptoms:**
- How long has this been going on?
- What does a typical day look like energy-wise?
- Any particular time of day when things are better or worse?
- What has changed in the last 1-3 years?

**Previous testing:**
- What blood tests have you had done, NHS or private?
- What were you told about the results?
- Was there anything that came back slightly off, even if they said it was fine?
- Have you ever had fasting insulin tested? (Most people: no)

**Lifestyle context:**
- What does your diet look like broadly?
- Sleep quality and quantity?
- Exercise, roughly how often and what kind?
- Any significant stress or life changes recently?

**Weight and metabolic:**
- Has your weight changed significantly in the last few years?
- Any family history of diabetes, cardiovascular disease, or thyroid issues?

**Goals:**
- If you could leave this year knowing three things about your health that you do not know right now, what would they be?

[INTERNAL NOTE: you are listening for the gap between what they know and what is actually happening. The bigger the gap, the stronger the fit.]

---

### 15:00-22:00 — CLINICAL FRAMING (7 minutes)

Based on what they have shared, reflect back the clinical picture:

"What you are describing is [a pattern I see quite commonly / something specific I want to explain].

The standard NHS panel is designed to catch established disease. It does that reasonably well. What it is not designed to do is pick up early dysfunction, and that is exactly where people sit for years feeling that something is off.

The markers that most commonly explain what you are describing include [relevant 2-3 markers based on their history: fasting insulin for energy/weight, ApoB/Lp(a) for cardiovascular concern, thyroid for fatigue/brain fog, etc.]. None of those are on a standard NHS screen.

What we do in the Metabolic Turnaround is test all of them properly, and then I sit with you for an hour and explain exactly what your pattern means, not just whether numbers are in range but what they mean for your specific physiology and what to do about it."

[DESCRIBE THE PROGRAMME BRIEFLY:]

"The programme runs for 90 days. It starts with the blood panel, then we do a full interpretation session where I take you through every relevant result. From that I build your personalised plan. Then we check in every two weeks so I can see how you are responding and adjust.

The investment is £1,895, or you can spread it as three payments of £695. That includes the blood panel, all five check-ins, and the written plan."

---

### 22:00-26:00 — QUESTIONS AND OBJECTIONS (4 minutes)

"What questions do you have for me?"

[Common objections and responses:]

**"I have had private blood tests before."**
"What did you get back from them? [...] The difference is not really the panel, though ours covers things most panels skip. It is the interpretation and the 90-day clinical relationship. A result on its own is just a number. Understanding what the pattern means and having someone adjust your plan as you go is different."

**"Is this similar to functional medicine?"**
"I practice evidence-based preventive medicine, which means I use the same clinical evidence base as mainstream medicine but I focus on markers that conventional practice runs infrequently or not at all. I do not use speculative diagnoses or unvalidated tests. Everything in the panel has a clear clinical rationale."

**"Is the price negotiable?"**
"The programme is priced to reflect the level of clinical time involved. What I can do is structure it across three payments if that is helpful. The split payment option is there specifically for that."

**"I want to think about it."**
"Of course, there is no pressure. What I would suggest is that if you have any further questions after today, you can email me directly at dr.tosin@veridianclinic.com and I will answer them personally. The only thing I would say is that we take ten patients per month, and places do fill. But take the time you need."

**"Will this actually work?"**
"I can tell you what the programme covers and what the evidence supports. What I cannot promise is a specific outcome, because medicine does not work that way. What I can promise is that you will leave with a far clearer picture of your metabolic health than you have right now, and a specific plan built from your actual results. That is the Complete Picture Guarantee we offer."

---

### 26:00-30:00 — CLOSE AND NEXT STEPS (4 minutes)

**If the fit is clear:**

"Based on what you have described, I think this programme is a good fit for you. Here is what I would suggest: I will send you a follow-up email within the next couple of hours that covers the programme overview, the payment options, and the link to get started. If you have any more questions after reading it, reply directly and I will come back to you.

Does that work for you?"

**If the fit is unclear:**

"I want to be honest with you. Based on what you have described, I think [the programme is a good fit / there is a more straightforward step first / a different pathway might suit you better]. Here is what I would suggest..."

[Do not push people into a programme that is not right for them. Reputation depends on appropriate clinical selection.]

**If clearly not the right fit:**

"I appreciate you taking the time to speak with me. Based on what you have told me, I do not think the Metabolic Turnaround is what you need right now. What I would suggest instead is [specific recommendation]. I am happy to point you in the right direction."
